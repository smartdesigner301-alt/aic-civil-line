Our Campus

AIC Civil Lines fosters an inclusive, internationally minded learning community where young children learn through play, inquiry, and exploration. Guided by the IB philosophy, we prioritize each child’s holistic development, health, and well-being, fostering curiosity, confidence, and a balanced approach to lifelong learning.

Located in Faisalabad, AIC Civil Lines offers a purpose-built campus designed to meet the learning and developmental needs of Early Years Learners (EYLs) and Primary Years Programme (PYP) students. Students are encouraged to engage with their surroundings, participate in meaningful learning experiences, and develop a sense of belonging within an inclusive community that values curiosity, innovation, and lifelong learning.

Student Life

At AIC Civil Lines, student life is a vibrant blend of learning, creativity, and meaningful experiences. Students build lifelong friendships while engaging in activities that reflect their interests, passions, and perspectives.

Special Interest Groups (SIGs)

Alongside inquiry-based learning, students engage in swimming, Taekwondo, debate, drama, and music, developing physical well-being, confidence, communication, creativity, and collaboration in alignment with the IB philosophy.

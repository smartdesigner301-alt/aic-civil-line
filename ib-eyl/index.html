Where Learning Begins with Joy

At AIC Civil Lines, the Early Years Learning (EYL) programme is designed to honor the natural curiosity, imagination, and agency of young children. Guided by the International Baccalaureate philosophy, we understand that play is the primary vehicle through which young children make sense of the world.

Sensory & Texture Investigation

Children explore materials, colors, and textures to build tactile discernment and scientific curiosity.

Playroom Collaboration & Construction

Interactive block building and spatial problem-solving that foster peer teamwork and early engineering concepts.

Developmental Focus Areas

Four foundational pillars shaping our daily early childhood experiences.

Sensory & Cognitive Exploration

Hands-on discovery through textured materials, shape identification, sorting, and exploratory curiosity.

Social & Emotional Well-Being

Cultivating empathy, collaboration, respect for peers, and self-regulation in a joyful classroom environment.

Creative Expression & Play

Music, role-play, drawing, finger painting, and building blocks that allow imagination to flourish without limits.

Foundational Literacy & Numeracy

Language-rich experiences, rhymes, phonemic games, and interactive counting through purposeful play.

Dedicated Faculty

Caring, One-on-One Mentorship

Our IB-trained Early Childhood educators provide individualized attention to each learner. With a low student-to-teacher ratio, educators observe, guide, and scaffold each child’s unique journey, honoring their questions and celebrating every milestone.

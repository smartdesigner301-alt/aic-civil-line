Inquiry That Drives Real Understanding

The International Baccalaureate Primary Years Programme (IB PYP) at AIC Civil Lines offers an inquiry-based, transdisciplinary curriculum framework that challenges students to think for themselves, explore concepts in depth, and take responsible action in their communities.

Capstone Milestone

The PYP V Exhibition

In their final year of primary school (PYP V), students conduct an extensive, collaborative inquiry into a real-world issue—such as environmental sustainability, technological ethics, or social equity. They formulate central ideas, conduct interviews, synthesize data, and present their findings to parents, teachers, and community members.

6 Transdisciplinary Themes

Rather than learning subjects in isolation, students explore six overarching themes that span language, mathematics, social studies, and natural sciences.

Student Agency & Leadership

Through the Student Council, House Captains, and Prefects, learners exercise real responsibility, helping shape school initiatives and organizing community events.

Co-Curricular & Special Interest

Integrated sports and arts programmes—including Taekwondo belt certifications, swimming academy, debating tournaments (ADC), and musical theatre.

The IB Learner Profile

Attributes that our curriculum actively nurtures in every student.

Inquirers

Developing their natural curiosity and acquiring research skills.

Knowledgeable

Exploring concepts and issues with local and global significance.

Thinkers

Exercising initiative in applying thinking skills critically and creatively.

Communicators

Expressing ideas confidently and collaborating effectively.

Principled

Acting with integrity, honesty, and a strong sense of fairness.

Open-minded

Appreciating their own culture and open to the perspectives of others.

Caring

Showing empathy, compassion, and respect toward others.

Reflective

Giving thoughtful consideration to their own learning and strengths.

Milestones

Celebrating Every Achievement

From their first inquiry presentation in Early Years to their Prep and Primary graduation ceremonies, we celebrate learners’ academic growth, character development, and personal breakthroughs.

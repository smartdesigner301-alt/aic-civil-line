IB Learning Continuum

Purposeful Education Rooted in Inquiry

At Angels International College (AIC) Civil Lines, Faisalabad, academic excellence is rooted in the philosophy of the International Baccalaureate (IB). We offer an inclusive, child-centered environment that fosters conceptual understanding, critical inquiry, and international-mindedness from early childhood through primary school.

Ages 3 – 5

IB Early Years Learning (EYL)

Play-based inquiry that nurtures wonder, sensory exploration, and motor skills. Children explore textures, materials, numbers, and foundational language in stimulating indoor and outdoor environments.

Explore IB-EYL Programme
Ages 6 – 11

IB Primary Years Programme (PYP)

Inquiry-driven conceptual education focused on global citizenship, transdisciplinary themes, collaborative investigations, and the culminating PYP V Exhibition.

Explore IB PYP Programme

Pillars of Academic Excellence

How our teaching and learning philosophy translates into daily student success.

Inquiry-Led Discovery

Students learn to ask meaningful questions, research independently, and connect conceptual knowledge to real-world experiences.

Transdisciplinary Learning

Curriculum transcends traditional subject silos, exploring themes that connect sciences, arts, languages, and humanities seamlessly.

Learner Agency & Voice

Learners take ownership of their learning, developing the confidence to make decisions, express viewpoints, and reflect thoughtfully.

Holistic Development

Academic rigor is balanced with physical well-being through swimming, martial arts, debate championships, drama, and musical arts.

IB Continuum Mission

“Our curriculum empowers students to become proactive, compassionate inquirers who see problems as opportunities to learn, create, and make a positive impact.”

About Angels International College

Established in 2022 in Faisalabad, AIC, Civil Lines is committed to nurturing young learners through the principles and philosophy of the International Baccalaureate (IB) Primary Years Programme (PYP), with a strong focus on early childhood development and primary education.

We provide a nurturing, inquiry-driven, and inclusive learning environment where curiosity is encouraged, individual strengths are celebrated, and learning is connected to real-life experiences. Through play-based exploration, conceptual understanding, and purposeful inquiry, we empower children to become confident, independent thinkers who are caring, principled, and open-minded.

“AIC, Civil Lines nurtures curious, confident, and compassionate young learners through the IB PYP, fostering inquiry, creativity, international-mindedness, and a lifelong love of learning.”
